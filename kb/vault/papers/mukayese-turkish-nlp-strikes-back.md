## Claims

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-turkish
> [tr] Mukayese (ACL 2022 Findings, tdd-ai) remains the foundational Turkish NLP benchmarking platform — 18 baseline models across 8 tasks (spell-checking, sentence segmentation, LM, NLU) with 4 newly released benchmarking datasets — predating the 2024-26 wave of TurkishMMLU/Cetvel/TR-MTEB and establishing the 'benchmark-suite-per-language' template those later works followed.
>
> **Numbers:** 18 baseline models, 8 tasks, 4 new datasets; ACL 2022 Findings
> **Relevance:** Historical anchor showing Turkish's benchmark infrastructure build-out took ~4 years (Mukayese 2022 to Cetvel/TabiBERT 2025-26) — sets a realistic multi-year timeline expectation for the Kazakh atlas's own benchmark-maturity trajectory, not a one-paper sprint.
