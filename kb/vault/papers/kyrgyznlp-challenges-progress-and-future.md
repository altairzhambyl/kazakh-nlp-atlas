## Claims

> [!warning] UNCERTAIN — turkic-kyrgyz-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-land
> [ky] Independent scientometric review of Kyrgyz NLP identifies the field as 'Scraping By' (severely under-resourced tier) based on analysis of 67 research works since 2011; existing corpora are fragmented: Manas-UdS (1.2M words, 84 literary texts), kyWaC (19M words, crawled Jan 2012), Leipzig Corpora Collection (news+community crawls, 'hundreds of thousands' of sentences), and TilCorpusu (reported variously as ~100M or 300M words, made public 2023) — no single consolidated large corpus existed until KyrText (2026).
>
> **Numbers:** 67 papers reviewed since 2011; Manas-UdS 1.2M words; kyWaC 19M words; TilCorpusu 100M words (one description) OR 300M words (conflicting description) [UNVERIFIED which is correct]
> **Relevance:** Confirms Kyrgyz's fragmentation problem pre-2026 — the field lacked a single go-to corpus, which is why KyrText (680.5M words) is a meaningful step-change and why the lab should treat any pre-2026 Kyrgyz numbers with caution.
> **Source:** arXiv:2411.05503 (KyrgyzNLP: Challenges, Progress, and Future, 2024) · **Sweep:** `turkic-landscape`

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-kyrgyz-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-land
> [ky][kk][tt][ug][uz][alt] Lexical similarity (Swadesh 215-word list) of Kyrgyz to sibling Turkic languages: Kazakh 91%, Tatar 79%, Uyghur 77%, Uzbek 76%, Altai 73%. Also: a single Kyrgyz verb can take 'thousands' of inflected forms vs a maximum of ~150 for a Russian verb.
>
> **Numbers:** Kazakh 91%, Tatar 79%, Uyghur 77%, Uzbek 76%, Altai 73% lexical similarity to Kyrgyz; Kyrgyz verb forms 'thousands' vs Russian ~150
> **Relevance:** The 91% Kazakh-Kyrgyz lexical similarity is the single number that explains BOTH the langID-contamination problem (finding above) AND why cross-lingual transfer experiments (KB: 'Left Behind' arXiv 2603.21036) show Aya Expanse emitting Kyrgyz output for Kazakh prompts — it is the highest cross-language similarity in the whole family, making kk<->ky the most transfer-prone AND most contamination-prone pair the lab will encounter.
> **Source:** arXiv:2411.05503 (KyrgyzNLP challenges paper) · **Sweep:** `turkic-landscape`
