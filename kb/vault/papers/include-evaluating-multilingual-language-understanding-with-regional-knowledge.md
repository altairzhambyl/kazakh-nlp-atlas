> [!warning] UNCERTAIN — turkic-pan-benchmarks-non-kazakh-turkic
> Global-MMLU includes Kyrgyz [ky] and Turkish [tr] but NOT Azerbaijani, Kazakh, or Uzbek. INCLUDE benchmark (arXiv:2411.19799, 44 languages/1,926 exams/197,243 QA pairs) includes Azerbaijani [az] and Kazakh [kk] but not Uzbek or Kyrgyz.
>
> **Numbers:** Global-MMLU: ky+tr only among Turkic; INCLUDE: az+kk only among Turkic (of 44 total langs, 197,243 QA pairs)
> **Relevance:** No single mainstream multilingual-knowledge benchmark covers Kazakh AND Uzbek AND Kyrgyz together except TUMLU — reinforces TUMLU as the only viable cross-Turkic protocol anchor for a design spec that must compare Kazakh against Uzbek/Kyrgyz on the same instrument.
