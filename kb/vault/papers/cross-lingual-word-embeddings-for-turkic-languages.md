## Claims

> [!warning] UNCERTAIN — turkic-uzbek-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-lands
> [pan-turkic] Kuriyozov, Doval & Gómez-Rodríguez (arXiv:2005.08340, 2020) built cross-lingual FastText word embeddings aligned across multiple Turkic languages including Uzbek, using a 24M-word Uzbek web-crawled corpus and a ~200K-word-vocabulary model, aligned against embeddings for other Turkic languages.
>
> **Numbers:** Uzbek training data 24M words; model vocab ~200K words
> **Relevance:** Establishes that 'align embeddings across the Turkic family' is not a new idea — it has a 2020 static (FastText, non-contextual) precedent. Any pan-Turkic contextual/transformer embedding claim from the lab must differentiate against this prior art, not just against TR-MTEB.
