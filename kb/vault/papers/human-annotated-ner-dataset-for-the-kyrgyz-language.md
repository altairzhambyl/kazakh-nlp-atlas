# Human-Annotated NER Dataset for the Kyrgyz Language

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19109)
**Topics:** [[turkic-kyrgyz-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-land]]

> [!abstract]
> We introduce KyrgyzNER, the first manually annotated named entity recognition dataset for the Kyrgyz language. Comprising 1,499 news articles from the 24.KG news portal, the dataset contains 10,900 sentences and 39,075 entity mentions across 27 named entity classes. We show our annotation scheme, discuss the challenges encountered in the annotation process, and present the descriptive statistics.

## Claims

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-kyrgyz-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-land
> [ky] KyrgyzNER is the first manually-annotated Kyrgyz NER dataset: 1,499 news articles (24.KG portal), 10,900 sentences, 39,075 entity mentions across 27 entity classes. Multilingual RoBERTa variants achieved the best precision/recall balance in baseline evaluations; all tested models struggled on rare entity categories.
