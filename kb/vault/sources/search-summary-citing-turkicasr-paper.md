## Findings

> [!warning] UNCERTAIN — turkic-pan-models
> [pan-turkic] TurkicASR (multilingual ASR across 10 Turkic languages incl. Bashkir [ba], Tatar [tt], Turkish [tr], Uyghur [ug], Uzbek [uz]) achieves 56.7% relative CER reduction via cross-lingual transfer compared to monolingual baselines; TurkicTTS (ISSAI) covers 10 lower-resourced Turkic languages (Azerbaijani, Bashkir, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Sakha, Tatar, Turkish, Turkmen, Uyghur, Uzbek).
>
> **Numbers:** 56.7% relative CER reduction from cross-lingual transfer
> **Relevance:** Speech-side evidence that cross-Turkic transfer is empirically strong (>50% relative error reduction) — supports porting the lab's evaluation protocols to sibling Turkic languages as a low-risk, well-precedented move, though this is ASR not text-embedding/retrieval so the transfer coefficient may not carry over directly.
