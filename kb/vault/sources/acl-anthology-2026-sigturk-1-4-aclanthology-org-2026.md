## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-landscape-prior-ar
> [tr][az][uz][ky][kk][pan-turkic] A live, dedicated pan-Turkic embedding-retrieval benchmark paper exists and directly documents which languages are covered/excluded: 'A Unified Turkic Idiom Understanding Benchmark: Idiom Detection and Semantic Retrieval Across Five Turkic Languages' (Aslantaş & Güngör, SIGTURK 2026, ACL Anthology 2026.sigturk-1.4) evaluates 9 embedding models on semantic retrieval across Turkish, Azerbaijani, Turkmen, Gagauz, and Uzbek. Kazakh and Kyrgyz are confirmed NOT included in the language set.
>
> **Numbers:** 5 languages: tr, az, tk, gagauz, uz; 9 embedding models evaluated; Kazakh (kk) and Kyrgyz (ky) explicitly absent
> **Relevance:** Direct evidence for Decision 3: even the most recent (2026) dedicated pan-Turkic embedding-retrieval benchmark deliberately excludes Kazakh AND Kyrgyz, confirming these two languages remain genuinely unclaimed territory for a lab retrieval-benchmark artifact, while Turkish/Azerbaijani/Uzbek/Turkmen retrieval is already being actively benchmarked by a competing group.
