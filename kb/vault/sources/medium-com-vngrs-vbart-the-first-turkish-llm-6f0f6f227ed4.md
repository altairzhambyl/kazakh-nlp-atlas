## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-turkish
> [tr] VBART (VNGRS, Jan 2024, precursor to Kumru) achieves 1.33 tokens/word with a 32K-vocab SentencePiece Unigram tokenizer trained on only 10GB of Turkish text, vs 3.10 tok/word for OpenAI's BPE tokenizer on the same text (2.33x more compact). VBART-Large (374M, mBART architecture) matches mT5-Large (1.2B, 3.2x more params) on Turkish question generation/answering.
>
> **Numbers:** 1.33 tok/word (VBART) vs 3.10 (OpenAI BPE), 2.33x compaction; 32K vocab; 10GB tokenizer-training text; VBART-Large 374M matches mT5-Large 1.2B
> **Relevance:** Turkish's achievable fertility floor (1.33) is even better than Sherkala's tuned Kazakh fertility (2.04) or the lab's <2.0 target — confirms Turkish's simpler morphology-to-token mapping (or smaller effective vocabulary need) gives it a structural tokenization advantage Kazakh cannot fully close even with a dedicated tokenizer.
