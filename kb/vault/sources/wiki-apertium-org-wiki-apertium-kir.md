## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-kyrgyz-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-land
> [ky] Apertium-kir provides a 'Stable'-quality finite-state morphological analyzer/generator (Kymorph) for Kyrgyz built with HFST, originally developed for a Turkish→Kyrgyz MT prototype, and Apertium ships a dedicated apertium-kaz-kir bidirectional Kazakh↔Kyrgyz translation pair (leveraging the two languages' high mutual intelligibility).
>
> **Numbers:** qualitative (no error-rate numbers published)
> **Relevance:** Confirms rule-based morphological tooling for Kyrgyz already exists and is architecturally compatible with Kazakh's own Apertium morphology work — a cheap starting point if the lab ever wants a morphology-aware tokenizer ablation ported to Kyrgyz.
