> [az][crh][kaa][kk][ky][tt][tr][ug][uz] TUMLU / TUMLU-mini (Isbarov et al., arXiv:2502.11020, ACL 2025) is a NATIVE (non-machine-translated) MMLU-style benchmark spanning 9 Turkic languages, sourced from real university-entrance-exam questions, manually verified by native speakers, 7-11 subjects. TUMLU-mini = 6,017 examples total (700/lang mostly, less for low-resource: Crimean Tatar 417, Karakalpak 315, Uyghur 494, Kyrgyz 698). 5-shot results (mean across all 9 langs, then per-lang for kk/tr/uz): Claude 3.5 Sonnet mean 78.9 (tur 85.7 highest, tat 84.0, aze 84.4, kaz 83.0, kir 75.7, crh 81.2, uig 71.3, kaa n/a in this row set, uzb 69.1 LOWEST); GPT-4o mean 74.9 (tur 83.7, kaz 81.0, uzb 65.4); Qwen2.5-72B mean 59.9 (tur 73.9, kaz 62.6, uzb 50.4, kir 47.5 lowest for this model).
