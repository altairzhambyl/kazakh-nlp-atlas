## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-landscape-prior-ar
> [tr] Turkish already has a mature, comprehensive MTEB-style benchmark: TR-MTEB (Baysan & Güngör, EMNLP 2025 Findings, aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.471) covers 6 core embedding tasks (classification, clustering, pair classification, retrieval, bitext mining, STS) across 26 datasets, plus a released Turkish embedding model suite and training corpus/scripts.
>
> **Numbers:** 6 task categories; 26 datasets; published EMNLP 2025 Findings
> **Relevance:** Confirms Turkish is NOT unclaimed territory for a kk/uz-MTEB-style artifact -- it already has the most mature benchmark in the family, one full evaluation generation ahead of any Kazakh effort; the lab should port protocols toward Uzbek/Kazakh/Kyrgyz, not Turkish.
> **Source:** ACL Anthology 2025.findings-emnlp.471; ITU research portal (research.itu.edu.tr) · **Sweep:** `turkic-landscape`
