## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-pan-benchmarks-non-kazakh-turkic
> TR-MTEB [tr] (ACL Findings EMNLP 2025) is the first large-scale Turkish embedding benchmark: 6 tasks (classification, clustering, pair classification, retrieval, bitext mining, STS), 26 datasets combining native + translated resources (msmarco-tr, quora-tr, nfcorpus-tr, scifact-tr among them). Authors built a 34.2M-pair weakly-supervised Turkish sentence-pair corpus and trained 2 Turkish-specific embedding models (contrastive pretrain + SFT) that 'significantly improve upon baseline monolingual models' and are competitive with multilingual baselines. HF org trmteb hosts 10 embedding models (incl. multilingual-e5-base fine-tuned variants, BERTurk contrastive/fine-tuned) and 37 datasets.
>
> **Numbers:** 6 tasks, 26 datasets (benchmark) / 37 datasets (HF org), 34.2M training pairs, 10 released models
> **Relevance:** TR-MTEB is the closest existing structural template for the lab's planned kk-MTEB: same task taxonomy (classification/clustering/pair-class/retrieval/bitext/STS) the lab should mirror for KazQAD-adjacent kk-MTEB construction, and 34.2M pairs sets a rough scale target for a Kazakh weakly-supervised pair corpus. No equivalent MTEB-style benchmark exists yet for Kazakh, Uzbek, Kyrgyz, Azerbaijani, or Tatar — kk-MTEB and a hypothetical uz/az-MTEB remain fully unclaimed territory.
