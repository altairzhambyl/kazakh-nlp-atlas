## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-landscape-embeddin
> [pan-turkic] Existing pan-Turkic evaluation resources found (mteb/TurkicClassification on HF; TUMLU benchmark covering 9 Turkic languages — az, crh, tr, ug, uz, kaa, ky, kk, tt — with 38K multiple-choice questions) are classification/knowledge-QA benchmarks, NOT retrieval/embedding benchmarks. No pan-Turkic retrieval or embedding-specific benchmark (i.e., a Turkic-family analogue of KazQAD or TR-MTEB's retrieval split, spanning multiple Turkic languages at once) was found to exist as of this sweep.
>
> **Numbers:** TUMLU: 9 languages, 38K MC questions (knowledge/language-understanding, not retrieval)
> **Relevance:** For decision (3): the retrieval axis of pan-Turkic benchmarking is unclaimed territory family-wide, not just for Kazakh — even Turkish (the best-resourced Turkic language) only has a single-language retrieval benchmark (TR-MTEB), and no pan-Turkic retrieval MTEB exists. A Turkic-wide retrieval benchmark (built on the lab's KazQAD-style protocol methodology) would be genuinely novel and could be a higher-leverage lab artifact than a Kazakh-only one — but is a much larger scope commitment.
> **Source:** HF dataset mteb/TurkicClassification; github.com/ceferisbarov/TUMLU; aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1112 (TUMLU paper, ACL 2025) · **Sweep:** `turkic-landscape`
