## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-kyrgyz-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-land
> [ky] KyrText (2026) is the largest consolidated Kyrgyz corpus to date: 680.5M words (2.33M documents) aggregated from News (61.4%, 1.9M docs), Court decisions (23.9%, 172.8K docs), Legislation (9.9%, 157K docs), Wikipedia (1.3%, Nov-20-2025 dump), and E-library/OCR'd literature (3.45%, using DeepSeek-OCR). Used for continual pretraining of mBERT/XLM-R-base/large/DeBERTaV3 and RoBERTa-from-scratch; best model xlm-roberta-large-kgz scores BoolQ 0.79, PAWS-X 0.85, XNLI 0.76, News-topic 0.92, SST 0.89 (all accuracy), a 7-19pp gain over the un-adapted xlm-roberta-large baseline.
>
> **Numbers:** 680.5M words total; 2,334,281 docs; SentencePiece Unigram tokenizer vocab 64,000; xlm-roberta-large-kgz: BoolQ 0.79 (+7pp), PAWS-X 0.85 (+18pp), XNLI 0.76 (+19pp) vs base xlm-roberta-large
> **Relevance:** Gives a concrete Kyrgyz-scale ceiling (680.5M words ≈ under 1B tokens) for what a from-scratch/continual-PT Kyrgyz effort can currently draw on — roughly an order of magnitude below the lab's own ~9-10B Kazakh token target, useful as a lower calibration point for the 'how much data does X Turkic language have' comparison table.
