## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-uzbek-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-lands
> [uz] TahrirchiBERT-base: 110M-param encoder-only Transformer, 30,528-token byte-level BPE vocab, Latin script only, MLM objective. Trained on ~5B tokens from Uzbek Crawl (1.2M curated documents) + Uzbek Books (~4,000 preprocessed books), 1M steps, batch 512, seq len 512. Apache 2.0 license. No benchmark scores published on the model card as of this sweep ('paper coming soon').
>
> **Numbers:** 110M params, vocab 30,528, ~5B tokens, seq len 512, 1M steps, batch 512
> **Relevance:** Direct Uzbek analog to SozKZ-scale monolingual pretraining; no reported downstream numbers means it cannot yet serve as a quantitative baseline for a joint kk/uz embedding effort — would need to be benchmarked first.
