## Findings

> [!warning] UNCERTAIN — turkic-embeddings-retrieval-non-kazakh-t
> [tr] SQuAD-TR (machine-translated SQuAD2.0 via Amazon Translate, boun-tabi, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) and STSb-TR (8,628 machine-translated STS-Benchmark pairs, 0-5 similarity scale, Beken Fikri et al. 2021) are the two most-used Turkish retrieval/STS resources feeding TR-MTEB's retrieval and STS tasks respectively — both are machine-translation-derived, and SQuAD-TR's NC-ND license blocks commercial reuse or redistribution of derivatives.
>
> **Relevance:** If the lab ever wants to reuse Turkish resources as auxiliary cross-lingual training signal for a Kazakh or pan-Turkic embedding model, SQuAD-TR's CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license is a hard blocker for any commercially-released model — STSb-TR's license was not confirmed and should be checked before reuse.
