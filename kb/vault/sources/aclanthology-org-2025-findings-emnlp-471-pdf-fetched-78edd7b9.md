## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-uzbek-non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-lands
> [tr] TR-MTEB (Baysan & Güngör, Boğaziçi University, EMNLP 2025 Findings, aclanthology 2025.findings-emnlp.471) is the FIRST large-scale, task-diverse Turkish sentence-embedding benchmark: 26 datasets across 6 task types (8 classification, 2 clustering, 3 pair classification, 1 bitext mining, 1 STS, 11 retrieval — including 7 BEIR datasets machine-translated into Turkish via Cohere aya-expanse-8B, validated with an LLM-judge pipeline reaching 85.2% agreement with human labels, precision 92.9%, recall 84.4%, F1 88.4% on a 115-example calibration set).
>
> **Numbers:** 26 datasets, 6 task types (8/2/3/1/1/11), 7 BEIR sets translated, LLM-judge: 85.2% agreement, precision 92.9%, recall 84.4%, F1 88.4% (n=115 calibration examples)
