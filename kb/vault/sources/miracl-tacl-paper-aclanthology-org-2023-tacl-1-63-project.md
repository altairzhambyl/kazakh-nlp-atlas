## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-non-kazakh-nlp-landscape-targeted
> [pan-turkic][uz][kk][tr] Standard multilingual open-domain QA/retrieval benchmarks that the lab could otherwise have relied on for Uzbek coverage all exclude both Uzbek and Kazakh: MIRACL (18 languages: ar, bn, de, en, es, fa, fi, fr, hi, id, ja, ko, ru, sw, th, yo, zh + 1 more) confirmed to exclude Turkish, Uzbek, and Kazakh entirely.
>
> **Numbers:** MIRACL: 18 languages, 726k relevance judgments, 78k queries over Wikipedia — 0 Turkic languages included
> **Relevance:** Confirms no 'free' multilingual retrieval benchmark already covers Uzbek by proxy; any pan-Turkic or Uzbek-specific KazQAD-style retrieval set must be built, not extracted from an existing multilingual resource — supports decision (1) build-first for Uzbek.
