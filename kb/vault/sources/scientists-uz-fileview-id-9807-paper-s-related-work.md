## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-embeddings-retrieval-non-kazakh-t
> [uz] Prior Uzbek NLP resources are monolingual-transformer-first, not embedding-first: UzBERT, UzRoBERTa, and BERTbek (monolingual BERT variants) 'have consistently outperformed multilingual models' on classification/NER, and SimRelUz (Kuriyozov et al. 2023) is the only standardized Uzbek semantic benchmark, but it is WORD-LEVEL similarity/relatedness, not sentence-level — AugSBERT-Uz had to manually adapt it into sentence pairs (e.g. wrapping word pairs into template sentences) for its evaluation, since no native sentence-level STS/NLI set exists for Uzbek.
>
> **Relevance:** Confirms Uzbek retrieval/STS eval infrastructure is far behind Kazakh's (KazQAD, KazParC): any Uzbek collaboration deliverable needs new eval-set construction as a first step, which is exactly the kind of upstream-mergeable artifact (native sentence-level Uzbek STS/retrieval set) that would fill unclaimed pan-Turkic territory (decision 3).
