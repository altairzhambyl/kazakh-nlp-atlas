## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — turkic-pan-benchmarks-non-kazakh-turkic
> Kardeş-NLU (EACL 2024) is the earlier cross-lingual transfer benchmark for 5 Turkic languages: Azerbaijani, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Uzbek, Uyghur [az][kk][ky][uz][ug], using Turkish as the high-resource 'donor' for intermediate-training and fine-tuning transfer experiments. General NLU tasks requiring no world knowledge; finds transfer gains are 'highly task- and language-dependent.'
>
> **Numbers:** 5 Turkic langs + Turkish donor; task-dependent gains (no aggregate % reported in abstract)
> **Relevance:** Establishes Turkish-as-pivot transfer as a validated strategy predating TUMLU; if the lab's Less-is-More embedding recipe needs a transfer donor for Uzbek, Kardeş-NLU's finding that transfer gains are task-dependent (not uniform) argues against assuming Turkish-pivot transfer will help retrieval specifically — it was only validated on NLU classification/inference tasks, not retrieval.
