## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — non-kazakh-turkic-nlp-landscape-embeddin
> [tr] The instruction-tuned variant multilingual-e5-large-instruct — often assumed to be the stronger/newer mE5 variant — actually scores WORSE than plain multilingual-e5-large on retrieval specifically in Turkish (57.21 vs 63.74, a 6.53-point gap), even though it wins some other task types (Classification 73.70 vs 71.77, Clustering 61.78 vs 60.42).
>
> **Numbers:** Retr.: mE5-large 63.74 vs mE5-large-instruct 57.21 (-6.53); Class.: 71.77 vs 73.70 (+1.93 for instruct)
> **Relevance:** The lab's canonical embedding target is plain mE5-large, not the instruct variant — this confirms that choice is retrieval-optimal and that the KB's Armenian/Belebele-kk numbers (which used mE5-large-instruct for some rows) should not be conflated with the base-model retrieval baseline the lab is trying to beat.
